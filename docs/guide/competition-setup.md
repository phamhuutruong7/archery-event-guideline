# Competition Setup & Scheduling

After successfully creating an event and registering athletes, the next step is to configure the competitions. A single event is often divided into multiple competitions based on equipment and gender (e.g., Recurve Men, Compound Women).

## 1. Understanding Competitions

Navigate to the **Competitions** tab within your Event Dashboard. Here you will see a list of all active competitions for your event. 

Selecting a specific competition will take you to the **Competition Dashboard**, where you manage the two main phases of an archery tournament:
1. **Qualification Round**
2. **Elimination Round**

## 2. Setup Qualification Shooting

The Qualification round is where athletes shoot a set number of arrows to determine their ranking and seeding for the Elimination round.

### Target Assignments

Before scoring can begin, the host must assign athletes to specific targets on the field.

1. Navigate to the **Schedule** or **Setup** section of your Competition.
2. Select **Qualification Setup**.
3. You will see a list of all registered athletes for this category.
4. **Assign Targets:** Enter the Target ID (e.g., "1A", "2B") for each athlete. 
   - *Tip: The application helps you organize athletes by ensuring no two athletes are assigned the exact same target position simultaneously.*
5. **Save Assignments:** Once all athletes are assigned, click save. 

This assignment dictates which digital scoresheet the athlete or their assigned referee will have access to during the round.

## 3. Setup Elimination Shooting

Once the Qualification round is complete and all scores are finalized, the system can determine the athletes' ranks. These ranks are used to seed the Elimination brackets.

### Generating Brackets

1. Ensure the Qualification round is marked as **Completed**.
2. Navigate to the **Elimination Setup** section.
3. Select the type of elimination format (e.g., Individual Elimination).
4. **Generate Bracket:** The system will automatically build the match tree. The #1 ranked archer will be placed against the lowest-ranked archer, the #2 against the second lowest, and so on, following standard World Archery bracket rules.
5. **Review Matches:** You can review the generated matchups before officially starting the Elimination round.

### Managing Byes
If the number of athletes is not a perfect power of two (e.g., 16, 32, 64), the system will automatically assign **Byes** to the top-seeded athletes, allowing them to advance automatically to the next round.
